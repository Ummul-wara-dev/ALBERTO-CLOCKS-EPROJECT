About Alberto Clocks

Our Story

Alberto Watch Company was founded with a simple vision: to offer the finest selection of luxury timepieces and exceptional repair services. Over the years, we have grown into a premier destination for watch enthusiasts. We carry top brands like Rolex, Michael Kors, Citizen, and Bulova. Our team of certified watchmakers is dedicated to preserving the art of horology.

Our Mission

We believe that a watch is not just a tool to tell time; it is a statement of style and a piece of art. Our mission is to help you find the perfect timepiece that matches your personality. We also specialize in restoring vintage watches to their former glory, ensuring that every tick tells a story of elegance and precision.
